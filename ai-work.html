Romeo and Juliet: Married to My Family's Enemy

Shakespeare's classic, reborn among rival Italian crime families. Mafia princess Juliet Capulet flees her arranged wedding at a Lake Garda beach club and meets a mysterious stranger. When her fiancé is killed, she is promised to the Montague heir — the same man from the beach, who knew who she was all along. Is he her protector, or the reason her fiancé is dead?

Gothic romance · Vertical series

Jane Eyre: The Nanny and the Billionaire's Secret Wife

Charlotte Brontë's classic, moved to modern Yorkshire. An orphan takes a job as a nanny at the remote estate of a rich, secretive man. She saves him from a fire in his bedroom — and finds signs of a hidden woman on the top floor. A story of passion, secrets and a marriage that is not what it seems.

Pride and Prejudice: The CEO's Secret Heir

Jane Austen's classic, reimagined for today. At a glittering masquerade, sharp-tongued waitress Elizabeth Bennet overhears billionaire CEO Fitzwilliam Darcy call her "not handsome enough to tempt me." Hours later she spends the night with a masked stranger. Six weeks on, she is pregnant and sold into a marriage with Darcy's unstable cousin — until Darcy finds the earring she left behind.
